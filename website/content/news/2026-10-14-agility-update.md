Agility has had a big update. Strap on your boots, because every rooftop course is now open.

## New courses

- **Draynor Village Rooftop Course** (level 1). Movement, animations and timings match live OSRS.
- **Gnome Stronghold Agility Course** (level 1): log balance, obstacle nets, tree branches, tightrope and pipes. 110.5 XP per lap including the 50 XP lap bonus, and the gnome trainers shout encouragement as you go.
- **All rooftop courses**, with current OSRS XP per obstacle:
  - Al Kharid (level 20, 216 XP per lap)
  - Varrock (level 30, 269.7 XP)
  - Canifis (level 40, 240 XP)
  - Falador (level 50, 586 XP)
  - Seers' Village (level 60, 570 XP)
  - Pollnivneach (level 70, 890 XP)
  - Rellekka (level 80, 780 XP)
  - Ardougne (level 90, 889 XP)

## Marks of Grace

Marks of Grace drop on every course and follow the OSRS rules: a 3-minute cooldown between marks, and an 80% lower chance once your base Agility is 20+ levels above the course. Canifis and Ardougne have double the usual chance, and Ardougne marks always spawn on the same tile.

**Grace** in the Rogues' Den sells the Graceful outfit and amylase packs for Marks of Grace (the full set costs 260 marks). You can also ask her to turn lap count messages on or off.

## Also new

- Failing rooftop obstacles now drops you to the ground for damage, and the chance shrinks as your Agility rises.
- Every rooftop obstacle restores 1-2% run energy.
- Lap counts for each course are shown in chat after each lap.

## Known issues

- Courses after Draynor haven't been checked against live OSRS yet, so expect small differences in animations, timings and mark spawn tiles.
- Achievement diary bonuses for rooftops aren't in yet.
