The new website is live. From now on, **accounts are created here** rather than on your first login.

## Getting started

1. [Register an account](/register) - pick a username (up to 12 letters, numbers and spaces) and a password.
2. Follow the [Play guide](/play) to set up the client.
3. Log in with the same username and password.

## What else is here

- **Hiscores** - see how you stack up in every skill.
- **Live player count** in the top bar.
- **News** - updates like this one, every time something ships.

Vote rewards and the store are coming soon.
