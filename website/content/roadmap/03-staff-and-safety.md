Moderation that works whether someone is online or not, with a paper trail for every action.

- [x] Staff admin area with authenticator sign-in
- [x] Kick, ban, mute and unmute with reasons
- [x] Punishment history and private staff notes
- [x] Player reports from the website and `::report` in game
- [x] Search by name, email or IP
- [x] Linked characters that share an address
- [x] Staff ranks and authenticator resets
- [x] Full audit log of every staff action
